Compliance

No Environmental Permit required at your site.

When fill material is placed as a certified by-product rather than waste, the receiving site needs no Environmental Permit. That is the central advantage of every deal we place.

The framework that makes this possible is the CL:AIRE Definition of Waste Code of Practice (DoW CoP), an EA-recognised process that formally confirms material is not legally classified as waste at the point of placement. Over 160 million cubic metres of material has been placed under this framework across the UK.

Every deal we place is fully documented under this framework. You receive the completed paperwork — your compliance record is covered.

CL
CL:AIRE DoW CoP Definition of Waste Code of Practice, the EA-recognised framework for by-product classification in UK construction
EA
Environment Agency aligned By-product route operates under Article 5 of the Waste Framework Directive, retained in UK law
